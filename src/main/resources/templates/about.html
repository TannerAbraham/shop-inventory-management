About Mountain Peak Guitar Shop

Our Story

Welcome to Mountain Peak Guitar Shop, your premier destination for high-quality guitars and parts. Founded with a passion for music and craftsmanship, we specialize in custom and pre-built electric and acoustic guitars made from premium components.

What We Offer

We carry a wide selection of guitar parts including strings, pickups, tuners, bridges, and amplifiers from top manufacturers. Our guitars are carefully assembled using the finest components to ensure superior sound quality and playability.

Our Commitment

At Mountain Peak Guitar Shop, we are committed to providing musicians with instruments that inspire creativity and deliver exceptional performance. Whether you're a beginner or a professional, we have the perfect guitar for your needs.
